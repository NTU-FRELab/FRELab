Our perspective

Reefs are built
from the bottom up.

Taiwan sits at the meeting point of tropical and subtropical seas—a natural laboratory for understanding how reefs function at their environmental margins.

FRELab connects organismal physiology, trait variation and demography with the structure and functioning of reef communities. We combine field ecology, experiments, photogrammetry, long-term monitoring and quantitative models across Taiwan and the Western Pacific.

Research

Across scales,
toward action.

Our work moves between mechanisms and patterns, from the performance of an individual colony to the trajectories of entire reef ecosystems.

01

Traits & performance

How physiology, trophic plasticity and individual variation shape the capacity of reef organisms to persist under changing conditions.

02

Reef communities

How biodiversity, habitat structure, interactions and energy flow combine to produce ecosystem properties across environmental gradients.

03

Change & conservation

How reefs respond to climate change and local pressures—and how monitoring, protected areas and restoration can support better decisions.
